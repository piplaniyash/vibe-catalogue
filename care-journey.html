Prototype

A care navigator for every family.

Cancer care in India is fragmented across hospitals, pharmacies, insurers and schemes, and families carry the coordination. This prototype shows how a navigator on WhatsApp could carry it with them.

Fictional families and data. Not medical advice.

What the navigator does

One point of contactA named navigator on WhatsApp, so the family never has to work out who to call.
Money firstSchemes, grants and insurance sorted early, because cost is what most often stops treatment.
Care at homeMedicine reminders, symptom check-ins and home visits between hospital trips.
Family in the loopUpdates for relatives in other cities or countries, without a dozen phone calls.
